Swapping crypto without an account

SimpleSwap, Changelly and ChangeNOW are swap services that exchange one coin for another straight between wallets — you pick a pair, they give you a deposit address, you send coin A and they send coin B to the address you supplied. How a no-account swap works

The mechanics are the same almost everywhere, because they have to be:

  1. You choose the pair (for example BTC → USDT on TRON) and the amount.
  2. You paste the destination address — where the output coin should land. For a casino deposit that is the deposit address the casino shows you, on the exact network the casino names.
  3. The service shows a quote and gives you a one-time deposit address plus an order ID.
  4. You send the input coin from your own wallet or exchange.
  5. After the input confirms on its chain, the service sends the output coin to your destination address.

There is no account, no password and no balance held for you. The order ID is usually your only handle on the transaction, so save it — if something goes wrong, that string is what support will ask for.

Fixed rate vs floating rate

Swap services generally offer two pricing modes, and the exact behaviour is defined in each service's own terms rather than by any shared standard:

  • Floating: you get whatever the market gives when your deposit confirms. Usually the cheaper headline rate, but the final amount is not known until the swap executes.
  • Fixed: the quoted rate is held for a short window. The spread is typically wider, and services normally state what happens if your deposit arrives after the window closes or the amount does not match the quote.

If the output has to cover an exact figure — a bonus minimum, say — read the fixed-rate terms carefully, because a few cents short is still short.

The refund address matters

Most of these services ask for a refund address, and some make it optional. It is worth filling in anyway. If a swap cannot be completed — wrong amount, expired quote, a pair that becomes unavailable — the refund address is generally how the input coin gets back to you. Several providers describe recovery without one as a manual support process. We have not tested how any individual service handles this, so check the wording on the order page before you send.

The same applies to the chain you pick. USDT exists on TRON, Ethereum, BNB Chain and others, and those are different assets as far as the network is concerned. Sending TRC-20 USDT to an ERC-20 address is not something a swap service can undo for you.

Where the cost hides

The quoted rate is rarely the whole price. Three things sit between you and the output amount: the network fee you pay to send the input coin, the service's spread versus the market rate, and whatever the service charges or deducts on the payout side. How that last one is presented — deducted from your output, added to your input, or shown as a separate line — varies by provider and by pair, so compare the final receive figure rather than the advertised percentage. Quoting the same pair on two or three services takes a minute and is the only honest way to see who is cheaper on that day.

FAQ

Can I swap crypto straight into a casino deposit address?

Technically yes — you set the casino's address as the swap destination. The safer habit is to swap into a wallet you control, confirm the amount and network, then deposit. If a swap arrives late or short, a wallet you control gives you room to fix it; a casino address does not.

Do SimpleSwap, Changelly and ChangeNOW require ID?

They advertise swaps without registration, and each publishes its own compliance policy covering when checks may apply. We have not verified those policies, so read the current terms on the provider's site before you send funds.

Which network should I choose for a USDT deposit?

Whatever the casino's cashier specifies. TRON (TRC-20) is widely used because fees are low and confirmations are quick, and it is supported at the casinos in our table above. Always match the network label exactly.

What if the swap sends less than I expected?

With a floating rate, a smaller output after a price move is normal behaviour rather than an error. If the shortfall is large or the output never arrives, contact the service with the order ID and the input transaction hash. There is no account to log into, so those two strings are your case.

Is swapping cheaper than buying the coin on an exchange?

It depends on the pair, the amount and the day. Exchanges often have tighter spreads but need an account and verification; no-account swaps charge for the convenience. For small casino deposits the difference is usually a few percent — compare the final receive amount, not the headline rate.
