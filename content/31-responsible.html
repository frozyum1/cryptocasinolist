Responsible gambling

Every casino on this site has a house edge. Over enough rounds, the house wins. Treat what you deposit as the price of entertainment, not an investment, and stop when it stops being fun.

Warning signs

  • Chasing losses with bigger bets or new deposits.
  • Gambling with money meant for rent, bills or debt.
  • Hiding how much you play from people close to you.
  • Feeling anxious or irritable when you are not playing.

Tools every casino here offers

Deposit limits, loss limits, session reminders, cool-off periods and self-exclusion. Set a deposit limit before your first deposit, when you are thinking clearly, not after a loss. Self-exclusion is permanent or long-term and cannot be reversed by support on request; that is the point.

Where to get help

  • Gambling Therapy, free online support in many languages.
  • Gamblers Anonymous, peer support meetings worldwide.
  • GambleAware, information and a helpline (UK).

You must be 18 or older, or the legal gambling age where you live, to use any casino listed here.
