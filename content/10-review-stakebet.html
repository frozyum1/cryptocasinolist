Deposits and withdrawals

StakeBet runs its own TRON wallet infrastructure for USDT, which is why that path is the fastest. Other coins route through a payment processor and take the usual network confirmations. There is no fiat on-ramp, so you need crypto already.

Withdrawals are queued for staff approval rather than auto-sent. In our test both were approved within minutes during European daytime; we would expect longer overnight. The upside is that the hot wallet is not automated, which is the usual failure point when a crypto casino gets drained.
