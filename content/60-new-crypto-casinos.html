How to vet a new crypto casino

  1. Check the domain before anything else. New brands attract clones, and clone sites copy the design pixel for pixel. Our guide to fake and clone casino sites covers the signals: lookalike spelling, no working support, deposit addresses that change between page loads.
  2. Verify a game result yourself. Provably fair is the one claim you do not have to take on trust. Paste a seed pair and a result into the provably fair verifier and see whether the hash matches. A site that cannot produce a server seed hash before the round is not provably fair, whatever the marketing says.
  3. Deposit small, then withdraw before you play seriously. A round trip of a minimum deposit tells you more about a new casino than any review. Keep the amount at a level you would not mind losing.
  4. Read the bonus terms before you accept. At many sites, including Lumenbet, taking a welcome bonus changes what you can withdraw and when.
  5. Check the KYC policy against your plans. Policies differ sharply; see the no-KYC comparison for who asks for documents and at what point.
  6. Withdraw to a wallet you control, keep one account per person, and do not use a VPN to get past a country block. Those three habits prevent most frozen-balance stories at any casino, new or old.
