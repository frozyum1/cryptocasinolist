Welcome match or VIP rewards?

Crypto casinos reward new players in two different ways. A welcome match adds bonus money on your first deposit, locked behind a wagering requirement. VIP and rakeback programmes give back a small share of what you wager, usually with no wagering at all, but they only become meaningful once you play a lot.

The large, established casinos such as Stake lean on VIP rewards and weekly races. Newer casinos compete with a bigger up-front match. Neither is a free lunch: the match costs you turnover, and rakeback is a fraction of the house edge you have already paid.

How to read a wagering requirement

A wagering requirement says how much you must bet before bonus money becomes withdrawable. A 30x requirement on a 100 USDT bonus means 3,000 USDT of bets. What that costs you depends on the house edge of the games you play:

GameTypical house edgeExpected cost of 3,000 USDT turnover
Coinflip-style originals1%30 USDT
Dice, Limbo, Mines, Plinko2%60 USDT
Crash3%90 USDT
Typical slot (96% RTP)4%120 USDT

So a 100 USDT bonus with 30x wagering is worth roughly 100 minus the expected cost: about 70 USDT on 1% games and about nothing on high-edge slots. That is an average, and variance is large either way, but it is the right way to compare offers. Check whether every game counts fully towards wagering; many casinos count originals or table games at a reduced rate.

Terms that matter more than the headline

  • Maximum bet while wagering. Breaking it, even once, usually voids the bonus and any winnings from it.
  • Time limit. Seven days for 30x wagering is a lot of play. If you cannot reach it, the bonus is worth zero.
  • Maximum cashout. Some offers cap what you can withdraw from bonus winnings. A low cap makes a large match pointless.
  • Separate or sticky balance. A separate bonus wallet lets you withdraw your own deposit at any time. A sticky bonus locks your deposit together with the bonus.
  • Limited offers. Some welcome bonuses are capped at a number of players; the table shows the spots left where the casino publishes it.
