Verification: what "level-based" means in practice

Stake's KYC policy on file is {{FACT:stake.kyc_note}}. Level-based verification means the account starts with limited privileges and unlocks more as you supply documents: typically an email and basic details first, then identity documents, then proof of address and sometimes source-of-funds questions at higher thresholds. The practical effect is that the gap between "I want to withdraw" and "the coins have left" is not only the blockchain — it is whatever review your current level triggers.

If you plan to cash out anything beyond pocket money, do the verification early rather than at the moment you are trying to withdraw. Documents requested mid-withdrawal are the single most common cause of a payout that feels stuck. If you want to avoid document uploads altogether, our no-KYC comparison sets out which policies exist and what each one really promises.
