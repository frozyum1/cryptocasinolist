Games: the originals are the draw

Stake lists {{FACT:stake.originals}} originals — games built around a published seed system rather than a slot studio's RNG. Crash, dice, mines, plinko and limbo style games all work on the same idea: a server seed is hashed and the hash is published before you play, you supply or receive a client seed, and the result is derived from both plus a nonce that increments each bet. When you rotate the seed, the server reveals the original string, and you can hash it yourself to confirm it matches the commitment you were given earlier. Nothing could have been changed mid-session without breaking that hash.

If you have never run that check, our provably fair verifier walks through it step by step with real seed values.

What verification does not do is change the house edge. Provably fair means "the result was not altered after you bet" — it does not mean the maths is neutral, and no seed check makes a negative-expectation game positive. We do not have Stake's per-game edge figures in our data, so we do not quote them; read each game's own rules page. For a sense of the scale involved, the edges StakeBet publishes for its originals run from 1% on coinflip, through 2% on dice, mines, plinko and limbo, 3% on crash, to 4% on keno. Over enough bets, that percentage of everything you wager is the cost of playing, wherever you play.
