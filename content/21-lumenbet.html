Why a mirror exists

  • Blocked networks. Some countries' internet providers block gambling domains. A second address reaches the same site when the first is filtered.
  • Advertising. Ad networks and partners sometimes require a dedicated domain for a campaign, so results can be measured per address.
  • Brand clarity. "StakeBet" is easily confused in search with Stake.com, a different casino. Lumenbet is a name nobody else uses.

How to tell a real mirror from a fake

  1. Only stakebet.gg, lumenbet.io and tazzoro.com are official. Any other domain using either name is not.
  2. Your login works on both. A site that asks you to create a new account "for the mirror" is not a mirror.
  3. The provably-fair verifier, the promotions and your balance are identical on both addresses.

Anything different on lumenbet.io?

Two small things. Telegram login is only offered on stakebet.gg, because Telegram ties its login button to one domain. Passkey (fingerprint or Face ID) logins created on stakebet.gg also only work there. Email, Google and MetaMask logins work on both.
