Provably fair games

BC.Game offers provably fair games. The mechanism is the same wherever it is implemented properly. Before you bet, the casino shows you the SHA-256 hash of a server seed it has already generated but is keeping secret. You supply or change a client seed. Each round combines the server seed, your client seed and a nonce that counts up one round at a time, and that combination produces the result. When you rotate your seed pair, the casino reveals the old server seed in plain text. You then hash that revealed seed yourself: if the hash matches the commitment you were shown earlier, the server could not have swapped the seed mid-session, and you can recompute every round from that seed pair to confirm the outcomes.

That check is worth doing once rather than trusting the badge. You can run it with our provably fair verifier, and compare which casinos publish verifiable games on our provably fair casinos page. Provable fairness covers round outcomes only — it says nothing about withdrawal policy, bonus terms or whether an account can be restricted.

Whatever the headline percentage is, the number that decides whether a bonus is worth taking is the rollover. If a bonus requires 30x wagering on the bonus amount, a 100 unit bonus means 3,000 units of qualifying bets before the balance can be withdrawn; at a 2% house edge, the expected cost of placing those 3,000 units of bets is about 60 units, which is a large slice of the bonus itself. Add a maximum bet cap and an expiry date and a generous-looking match can be harder to clear than a smaller one.
