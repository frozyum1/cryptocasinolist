What decides how fast you get paid

  1. Approval. Automated approval takes seconds. Manual approval depends on staff hours and queue length.
  2. Verification holds. A document request stops the clock until you respond. At casinos that verify before the first withdrawal, the first payout is the slow one.
  3. Bonus status. An active bonus with wagering left blocks or delays withdrawals almost everywhere.
  4. The network. Once sent, a transaction needs confirmations. This is where your choice of coin matters.

Casinos often review the first withdrawal on an account more closely: address checks, bonus checks and, at many casinos, identity verification.

Speed says nothing about safety on its own. Look at the operator's track record, licence status and whether its games can be verified, not only at payout time.
