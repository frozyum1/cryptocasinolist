The path a withdrawal takes

At any crypto casino, a payout moves through the same broad stages. Lumenbet publishes the window for the whole journey, not a breakdown per stage, so treat the stage list as the general mechanic rather than an internal timetable:

  1. Request. Wallet → Withdraw, choose the coin and the network, paste your address. Bonus funds still inside a wagering requirement are not withdrawable anywhere in this industry; that is the nature of a bonus balance.
  2. Review. Lumenbet states that a staff member approves withdrawals. We do not have a published description of what the reviewer checks or how long that step takes on its own, so we will not invent one.
  3. Broadcast. Once approved, the transaction is sent to the network and a hash exists.
  4. Confirmation. From that moment the blockchain sets the pace, not the casino.

The stage that varies is the review. A human step depends on staffing, and we have no published support hours for Lumenbet, so we cannot tell you which hour of the day has the shortest queue. What we can say is that the published window is a range, not a guarantee.

What "staff-approved" actually means

Manual approval is a genuine trade-off and it is worth being blunt about it. On the good side, a person reviewing outgoing transactions makes it harder for a compromised account to be emptied in one click, and it lets an operator run risk-based checks instead of demanding documents from every player. On the bad side, there is no guaranteed ceiling on how long you wait, and a request made outside working hours can sit until someone is back to review it.
