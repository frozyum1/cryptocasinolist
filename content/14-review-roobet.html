Provably fair, and what it does not cover

Roobet's in-house games are provably fair. The mechanic is standard across the industry: the casino commits to a server seed by publishing its hash before you bet, your client seed and a nonce are mixed in, and after the round the server seed is revealed so you can recompute the result yourself. If the hash matches and the result reproduces, that round was not altered after your bet was placed.

Two limits are worth knowing. First, provably fair proves a round was not tampered with; it does nothing to the house edge, which is built into the payout table and works against you over time regardless of how verifiable the round was. Second, it applies only to in-house games — third-party slots and live dealer tables run on the provider's own RNG, which is audited by a testing lab rather than verifiable by you. You can test the maths on any casino's seed pairs with our provably fair verifier, and see which sites expose verifiable games on our provably fair casinos page.
