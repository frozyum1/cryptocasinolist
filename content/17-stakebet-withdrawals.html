The path

  1. Request. Wallet → Withdraw, choose coin and network, paste your address. Bonus funds still in wagering cannot be withdrawn; your own deposit can.
  2. Automatic checks. Limits, velocity, and a risk score. Most requests pass in seconds.
  3. Staff approval. A person confirms the request. Daytime in Europe this was minutes for us; expect longer overnight.
  4. On-chain. USDT on TRON confirms in about a minute; Bitcoin can take 10 to 60 minutes; Ethereum a few minutes at variable fees.

What can add a check

  • A first withdrawal shortly after a large deposit with almost no play.
  • Several accounts on the same device or IP.
  • A withdrawal address that differs from the deposit source at unusual amounts.

In those cases StakeBet may ask for verification. That is the risk-based model: no documents for normal play, documents when something looks off.

Make it fast

  • Withdraw in USDT on TRON to a wallet you control.
  • Keep a few TRX in that wallet for moving the USDT on later.
  • Finish or forfeit bonus wagering before requesting.
