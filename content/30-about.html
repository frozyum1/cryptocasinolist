How we test

  1. Open an account without using any promotional code, so we see what an ordinary player sees.
  2. Deposit USDT on TRON, and at least one other coin where supported.
  3. Play the originals and a sample of slots for at least a week, checking the provably fair verifier on originals.
  4. Withdraw twice: a small amount early, and a larger amount later. Record request time, approval time and on-chain confirmation, and whether documents were requested.
  5. Read the bonus terms in full and note wagering multiples, expiry and game restrictions.

How we score

Five areas, weighted: money in and out (30%), verification (20%), fairness (20%), games (15%), rewards (15%). Scores are out of 10 and are revisited when a casino changes something material. The date at the bottom of each page is the last full review.
