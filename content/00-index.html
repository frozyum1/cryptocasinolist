How we scored them

Each casino was scored in five areas, weighted for how much they matter to someone playing with crypto rather than a card:

  • Money in and out (30%). Supported coins and networks, minimums, network fees passed on, and how long a withdrawal really took from request to on-chain confirmation.
  • Verification (20%). Whether you can deposit, play and withdraw without uploading documents, and what triggers a check.
  • Fairness (20%). Provably fair originals with a working verifier, published RTP on slots, and clear game rules.
  • Games (15%). Originals quality, slot providers, live tables, and anything unusual such as prediction markets or a sportsbook.
  • Rewards (15%). Whether bonuses pay in real, withdrawable balance and how heavy the wagering terms are.

We do not score bonus size. A large bonus with 50x wagering is worth less than a small one with none. See how we rate for the full method and our disclosure.

StakeBet does not require identity documents to deposit, play or withdraw; it runs risk-based checks instead and can ask for verification if a withdrawal looks unusual. Stake, BC.Game, Shuffle and Roobet all have verification levels that most players hit before their first sizeable withdrawal.

What is provably fair?

The casino commits to a hashed server seed before you bet, you supply a client seed, and the result is computed from both. After the round you can recompute it yourself. It proves the casino did not change the outcome after seeing your bet. It does not change the house edge.
