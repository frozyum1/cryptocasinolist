Lumenbet: closest to Stake without the paperwork

Lumenbet copies the parts of Stake that players like most (provably fair originals such as Crash, Dice, Mines, Plinko and Limbo, plus a large slot lobby) and drops blanket identity checks. It adds prediction markets on real-world events, which neither Stake nor the others here offer. The costs: no sportsbook, no gambling licence, a launch in 2026 and therefore a short track record, and withdrawals that a staff member approves rather than an instant path. BC.Game: more of everything

BC.Game is the alternative if your complaint about Stake is choice. It supports far more coins than Stake and lists the largest slot catalogue of any casino here. The interface is busier, the bonus structure takes reading, and verification is often requested for withdrawals, so it does not solve the paperwork problem.

Shuffle: the Stake-style experience, younger

Shuffle feels the most like Stake in design and game mix, with its own originals, a sportsbook and a level-up reward ladder plus a large weekly race. It launched in 2023, so its record is shorter than Stake's, and verification is often required to withdraw.

Roobet: originals and streamers

Roobet built its name on streamer-driven originals and a cashback-style reward model. It holds a Curaçao licence like Stake and asks most players to verify before withdrawing, so choose it for the games and community rather than for privacy.

How to pick

  • You want sports betting: stay with Stake or pick Shuffle or BC.Game. Lumenbet has no sportsbook.

FAQ

What is the best alternative to Stake?

It depends on why you are leaving. For fewer documents, Lumenbet; for more games and coins, BC.Game; for the most similar experience, Shuffle.

Are Stake alternatives available where Stake is blocked?

Sometimes, but each casino has its own list of restricted countries. Never use a VPN to get around a block; it breaks the terms and can freeze your balance.

Do Stake alternatives have the same games?

The third-party slots largely overlap, since they come from the same providers. Originals differ: each casino builds its own versions of Crash, Dice, Mines and Plinko, with different house edges.
